# Change the time zone

1. Open Parent Mode and go to Settings > Time zone.
2. Pick the new zone and save.

Effect: from then on, the time zone decides which calendar day counts as "today" (daily resets, streaks, history grouping). Dates and timestamps already stored do not move or get rewritten. Changing the zone near midnight can make "today" shift by a day immediately, so do it when the day's tasks are done.
