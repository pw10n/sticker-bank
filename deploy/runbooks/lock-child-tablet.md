# Lock a child's tablet to the app

Child Mode is open by design (no PIN), so keep the child inside the app at the device level.

## iOS / iPadOS: Guided Access
1. Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access: turn it on and set a passcode.
2. Open the installed Sticker Bank app, triple-click the side (or Home) button, and choose Guided Access > Start.
3. End a session by triple-clicking and entering the passcode.

## Android: screen pinning
1. Settings > Security (or Security & privacy) > App pinning / Screen pinning: turn it on, and enable "Ask for PIN before unpinning".
2. Open the app, open Overview (recent apps), tap the app icon at the top of its card, and choose Pin.
3. Unpin with the system gesture (Back + Overview, or swipe up and hold) and enter the PIN.

Menu names vary slightly by OS version and device maker.
